# 05 — Situational Adequacy Assessment (analytic-confidence gate)

This is the "状況判断としての適格性" check: before committing to a forecast, we
test whether the evidence is *adequate* to support a judgment, and at what
confidence. It applies the discipline of formal intelligence analysis (source
reliability, key assumptions, alternative hypotheses, confidence calibration).

## 1. Source reliability and currency

| Evidence stream | Reliability | Currency | Note |
|-----------------|-------------|----------|------|
| Historical base rates (midterm penalty, approval→seats) | High | Timeless | Strongest leg |
| Structural facts (thresholds, who's up, the map) | High | Stable | Facts, not estimates |
| **[LIVE] Presidential approval** (≈ −19.6 net) | High | **2026-05-29** | Multi-aggregator agreement |
| **[LIVE] Generic ballot** (D+6 to D+7) | High | **2026-05-29** | 3 aggregators within 1 pt |
| **[LIVE] Candidate recruitment** (Brown OH, Peltola AK) | High | **2026-05-29** | Reported as confirmed runs |
| **[LIVE] Redistricting net effect** (~+10 R) | Medium | **2026-05-29** | Estimate; maps in litigation |
| Seat-by-seat ratings | Medium-High | Mixed | Some live, some Jan-2026 |

**Improvement over first draft:** the most perishable inputs (approval, generic
ballot, key recruitments) were **refreshed live on 2026-05-29 via `WebSearch`**,
closing the four-month gap that the initial draft had to flag. Residual limits:
search returned *summaries* (not raw source pages — Wikipedia/538 fetches were
403-blocked), and the **redistricting seat estimate and final maps remain
genuinely uncertain** (active litigation). These are now the dominant
uncertainties, not data staleness.

## 2. Key assumptions (and what breaks the forecast if false)

1. **No president-favoring shock** (no 9/11- or impeachment-backlash-scale event
   reversing the mood). *If false → GOP could defy the base rate, as in 2002.*
2. **Approval stays deeply negative (net ≈ −15 to −20).** Strongly supported by
   live data; *a sharp recovery would shrink expected losses.* (Low risk — the
   trend in May 2026 was **down**, not up.)
3. **Redistricting nets Republicans ~10 House seats and the maps survive
   courts.** *If the cushion is larger / fully upheld, the House flip narrows; if
   courts strike GOP maps, the flip widens.* This is the **biggest swing factor.**
4. **Top-tier recruits (Brown, Peltola, etc.) remain on the ballot and
   competitive** through November.
5. **No major realignment in midterm turnout** beyond the expected out-party edge.

## 3. Alternative hypotheses (explicitly considered)

- **H1 (base case):** D flips House (clearly); R holds Senate. *Best supported.*
- **H2 (GOP holds House):** The ~10-seat redistricting cushion + incumbency
  absorbs the D+6-7 wave → R holds a tiny House majority; R holds Senate.
  *Plausible downside to H1; hinges on redistricting holding up.*
- **H3 (D wave / D takes Senate too):** −20 approval + D+6-7 persists, Democrats
  win Maine + Ohio + Alaska + NC and hold all their own → **D nets +4 and flips
  the Senate**, plus a larger House majority. *Live but uphill — needs a near-
  sweep of toss-ups.*
- **H4 (status-quo-ish):** Environment softens by autumn; R holds both narrowly.
  *Lower probability given the May trajectory.*

The strength of the live fundamentals **raises H1 and H3** and **lowers H4**
versus the January baseline; H2 persists **only because of redistricting.**

## 4. Confidence calibration

| Judgment | Confidence | Basis |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Republicans hold the Senate | **Moderate-High** | Map needs D+4; live recruits make it closer than before but still uphill for D |
| Democrats flip the House | **Moderate-High** | Live D+6-7 & −20 approval vs. ~10-seat GOP redistricting cushion |
| Specific seat counts / margins | **Low-Moderate** | Ranges only; redistricting + close races unresolved |

## 5. Adequacy verdict

With live May-2026 fundamentals incorporated, the evidence is **adequate to
support directional judgments at Moderate-High confidence** — a clear upgrade
from the staleness-limited first draft. It remains **inadequate for precise point
forecasts**, chiefly because the **redistricting seat math and final maps are
unresolved** and several races are true toss-ups. The forecast (file 06) is
therefore expressed as **direction + probability band + seat range + explicit
triggers**. Proceeding on that basis is well justified.
